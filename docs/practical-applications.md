# Practical applications

The course has twelve weeks. Arc 3 covers Weeks 7–8 and ends with a checked task; Arc 4 covers Weeks 9–12 and opens with temperature. Each week includes one `Apply it` section.

| Week | Example | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rewrite an email | Add the reader, purpose, action, and constraints |
| 2 | Open a morning shift at a drink shop | Ask for information that could change the decision |
| 3 | Define a good article | Compare examples, write with three criteria, and check the draft |
| 4 | Adapt a new-hire process for remote work | Separate what transfers from what changes |
| 5 | Improve a meeting summary | Compare fixed notes with a flawed draft; change one condition at a time |
| 6 | Compare vendors | Mark claims as supported, inferred, or unknown |
| 7 | Keep a brand voice | Separate context, memory, retrieval, and training |
| 8 | Try a task from start to finish | Define success, preserve sources, check each step, and test two cases |
| 9 | Name a product | Generate options, then select against criteria |
| 10 | Group customer feedback | Find repeated classification errors and retest one changed instruction |
| 11 | Find an exception in a return policy | Select the deciding passage and check its citation |
| 12 | Refine the Week 8 task | Review the two trials, explain mechanism choices, and test another case |

Week 8 integrates the former work-report application into the main lesson, preserving its notebook. It also supplies an email case, a complete-order comparison, and a changed-fee trial before the learner's own task. The lesson distinguishes supported facts, task suitability, and evidence of an external result.

Weeks 2, 4, 5, 8, 10, and 11 supply fictional source material. Learners can work through the cases without a live AI service, then try the method on their own tasks and save notes locally. The examples are teaching cases, not legal or workplace policy advice.

Storage follows content rather than the new week number: the temperature application in Week 9 keeps `week-8`; the report exercise in Week 8 keeps `week-9`; the new Week 8 task uses `arc3-task`. This preserves existing notes in both languages. The progress store migrates the old Week 8/9 checkpoint positions once.
